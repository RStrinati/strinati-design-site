Services

Design support from early feasibility through technical delivery.

STRINATI Design provides focused architectural and BIM consultancy for clients who need clear advice, coordinated information and practical delivery support.

01

Planning & feasibility

Early-stage site and project assessment, option testing, concept layouts, planning drawings and design information to establish what is viable before committing to the next stage.

  • Site and development feasibility
  • Concept and option studies
  • Planning / approval drawing packages
  • Existing-condition and measured information review
02

Detailed design & technical delivery

Developing design intent into coordinated, buildable information with attention to materials, interfaces, construction sequencing and the information needed by consultants and contractors.

  • Detailed architectural design
  • Technical drawing packages
  • Consultant coordination
  • Design development and construction-stage support
03

BIM & digital delivery

Practical BIM support informed by hands-on experience across architectural practice, construction and digital engineering. The focus is on improving coordination, information quality and delivery rather than adding process for its own sake.

  • BIM strategy and project setup
  • Revit / model health and coordination
  • ACC, Revizto and CDE workflows
  • Information requirements, model audits and digital delivery support
