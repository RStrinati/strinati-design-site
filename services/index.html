Services

Start with the decision you need to make.

Three practical starting points for residential alterations, additions and early-stage design. A commission can cover one defined package, with later stages agreed separately.

Fees are quoted against an agreed scope. Deliverables, information requirements, meetings, revision rounds and programme are set out in writing before work starts.

01

Site Potential Review

I want to understand what is possible.

An agreed review of the supplied property information, your objectives and the main constraints before committing to a full design commission.

What you receive
A written summary of opportunities, key questions and recommended next steps; an agreed discussion of the findings.
Scope boundaries
Surveying, measured drawings, detailed design, engineering, formal planning advice, applications and guarantees of approval are outside this initial review.
What helps us start
Preparing the address, available plans or survey, photographs, budget range and intended changes.
Ask about this package →
02

Concept & Layout Design

I know what I want to improve, but need a design.

Explore a renovation, addition or new-home brief through an agreed number of layout options and a preferred design direction.

What you receive
An agreed concept drawing set, presentation discussion and defined revision allowance. 3D views can be included where useful and agreed.
Scope boundaries
Approval submissions, construction details, consultant reports and additional options or revisions are separately scoped.
What helps us start
A project brief, available site information and a budget discussion; measured information may be needed before design starts.
Ask about this package →
03

Planning & Documentation

I need drawings for the next project stage.

Develop an agreed design into a clearly defined package for the relevant approval, consultant coordination or builder-pricing stage.

What you receive
A scheduled drawing set, agreed consultant interfaces, review points and revision allowance, matched to the stated purpose.
Scope boundaries
Council and certifier fees, surveys, specialist consultants, major redesign and construction-stage services are excluded unless expressly included.
What helps us start
The design status, previous approvals, survey and consultant information, required outputs and target programme.

How we work together

  1. Share the essentials. Tell Rico about the property, intended changes, budget range and timing.
  2. Agree a useful starting point. Check fit and availability, then agree the scope, fee and programme.
  3. Review the work. Discuss the outputs at the agreed review points and resolve the included revisions.
  4. Choose the next stage. Proceed only when the next scope, responsibilities and fee are agreed.

For builders and design teams

Defined drawing-production, design-development and coordination packages are also available by agreement. Send the required outputs, existing information and programme so the scope can be assessed.
