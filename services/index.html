Services

From site potential through planning, documentation and BIM delivery.

STRINATI Design provides outcome-focused architectural and technical support for homeowners, small developers, builders and design teams.

01

Site potential & planning

A focused review of the property, planning controls, constraints and opportunities, followed where appropriate by concept design and a coordinated planning package.

  • Site-potential and capacity testing
  • Concept and option studies
  • Planning / approval drawing packages
  • Development and planning coordination
02

Detailed design & documentation

Developing an approved or emerging design into coordinated information for consultants, builders and the next project stage, with drawing consistency and buildability kept in view.

  • Design-development support
  • Planning and construction drawing production
  • Consultant drawing and model coordination
  • Existing-condition and as-built documentation
03

BIM & technical delivery

Flexible digital-delivery support for builders, consultants and design practices requiring additional modelling, coordination or production capacity.

  • Revit modelling and BIM documentation
  • Drawing-set review and information management
  • Model coordination and project QA
  • Architectural visualisation and overflow drafting support

Engagement

Defined package, project stage or additional delivery capacity.

Scope, deliverables, software requirements, programme and review points are agreed before work begins so the support is clear and proportionate to the project need.
