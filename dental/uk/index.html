UK dental feasibility

Fast test-fit plans for proposed dental premises.

For practices assessing whether a property can support the clinical rooms, support spaces and circulation required before committing to a lease or purchase.

Best suited to

New practices, relocations, additional branches, lease negotiations and early NHS submission planning.

What to send

Existing floor plan or measured drawing, desired number of surgeries, key room requirements and any known property constraints.

What you receive

A clear feasibility layout showing a workable arrangement and the main issues that should be resolved before detailed design.

Next step

If the site is viable, the feasibility can form the basis for planning, approval or detailed fit-out work.
