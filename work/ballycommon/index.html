Overview

Testing development potential without overreaching the village context.

The project tested the development potential of a 0.65-acre site in Ballycommon, balancing yield, access, drainage, neighbouring amenity and the character of a low-density village setting.

The design process moved from planning research and site-capacity testing through multiple massing options, general arrangement, shadow and sun-hours analysis, public-realm strategy and residential unit development.

Design response

A measured four-house cluster.

The selected approach retains the porosity of the site, protects local views, provides generous green space and establishes a coherent relationship to Monsea Road. The proposal was developed alongside drainage and engineering input so the planning concept and technical strategy worked together.

Services: site-potential review, option studies, concept design, massing, planning drawings, sun/shadow analysis, drainage coordination, visualisation and unit planning.
