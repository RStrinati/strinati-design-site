What a Site Potential Review can tell you

Understand the purpose, outputs and limits of an initial review.

General project guidance · Reviewed November 2026

A first decision, not a finished design

A Site Potential Review helps you decide what is worth exploring. It considers the agreed property information, your priorities and the main questions that could influence a design direction.

Agree the questions before the review starts

The scope may focus on a renovation idea, an addition, layout potential or whether to progress to a concept stage. The information reviewed, outputs, discussion and any site visit are stated in the fee proposal.

What you take away

An agreed written summary of opportunities, constraints, information gaps and recommended next steps. Where a question needs a surveyor, planner, engineer, certifier or other specialist, that input is identified rather than assumed.

Understand the limits

The initial review is not a measured survey, structural assessment, formal approval, construction drawing set or fixed building-cost quote. It does not guarantee planning permission or establish every site constraint. Those boundaries keep the first engagement proportionate.
