Project guide

Design stages, drawings and fees

How to commission a manageable stage and understand what is included.

General project guidance · Reviewed November 2026

Commission the next useful stage

A project can begin with a review, move into concept design and then progress into a defined documentation package. You do not need to assume every stage is included in the first appointment.

Different drawings answer different questions

  • Concept drawings explore the brief, spatial options and preferred direction.
  • Approval documentation is prepared for a specified submission purpose and coordinated with the relevant supporting information.
  • Detailed documentation develops the agreed design for a stated purpose such as consultant coordination or builder pricing. Construction requirements must be expressly scoped.

Read the fee proposal alongside the deliverables

Check the drawing schedule, information assumptions, meetings, revision allowance, programme and exclusions. Ask which consultant and authority costs sit outside the design fee, and how a change to the brief will be handled.

Agree the next step in writing

Before proceeding, confirm what has been resolved and what remains open. This makes responsibilities clearer and helps avoid paying for detail before the main decisions are settled.
