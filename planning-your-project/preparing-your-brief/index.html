Project guide

Before your first design conversation

The information that helps turn an idea into a useful discussion.

General project guidance · Reviewed November 2026

Start with the change you want to make

You do not need a finished design brief. Describe what is not working, what you hope to improve and what you would like to keep. Light, space, storage, access and the way rooms connect are useful starting points.

Gather what you already have

  • The property address and a few photographs.
  • Existing plans, a survey or previous approvals, if available.
  • A short list of priorities, separating essentials from preferences.
  • Your approximate project budget and timing.
  • Any known site, access, neighbour or building constraints.

Missing information is a question to resolve, not a reason to delay an initial enquiry. Measured information or specialist input may be needed before design work begins.

Bring questions as well as ideas

Ask what the first stage will resolve, what you will receive, who else needs to be involved and which decisions can wait. The aim is to agree a useful scope before committing to a larger package.
