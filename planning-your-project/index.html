Common questions

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Start with what is not working in your home, what you would like to improve and any important constraints. An early review can help turn those priorities into a design brief.

Can I commission only a review or a layout package?

Yes. Work can be scoped around a defined initial package. Any later design or documentation stage is agreed separately.

How much will it cost?

Rico provides a written fee proposal based on the property information, required outputs and complexity. The proposal sets out inclusions, exclusions, meetings and revisions. Consultant and authority fees are identified separately where relevant.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on the scope, availability, quality of the starting information and any external inputs. A programme and review points are agreed before appointment; approval timeframes are outside the design programme.

Will my project need an approval?

That is a property- and proposal-specific question. The relevant approval pathway and any specialist input should be established before progressing a package for submission. An initial review does not guarantee an approval.

Are revisions included?

The agreed proposal identifies the included review points and revision allowance. Changes to the brief or additional rounds are discussed and priced before proceeding.

Can you help if I already have a builder or drawings?

Yes, subject to reviewing the available information, permission to use it and the required scope. Send the current drawings and explain the next output or decision you need.

Where do you work?

The residential focus is Sydney’s Eastern Suburbs, including Maroubra, Malabar, Coogee and nearby areas. Enquiries elsewhere can be considered against the scope and practical requirements.

How do I get in touch?

Message the business WhatsApp, call the Australian number or email. Include the suburb, intended changes and approximate timing. Reply timing is agreed around current project commitments; WhatsApp is not a live-chat service.
