About

Independent design practice with architectural and digital experience.

STRINATI Design combines architectural training, technical delivery and BIM/digital-delivery experience to help clients make clearer decisions earlier.

Approach

Useful design before unnecessary complexity.

The practice focuses on clearly defined problems: testing a site, developing a design, communicating an idea or improving a repetitive digital workflow. The aim is to provide enough information to move forward with confidence, then add detail where it creates value.

Architecture

Design, feasibility and technical thinking informed by work across architecture and construction.

Digital

BIM, model coordination, project data and automation experience used as practical design-delivery tools rather than ends in themselves.
