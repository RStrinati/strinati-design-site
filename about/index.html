Practice

Architectural thinking backed by technical and digital delivery experience.

STRINATI Design is an independent design consultancy led by Ricardo Strinati, combining architectural design, technical delivery and BIM expertise developed through practice and project work across Ireland, the UK and Australia.

Approach

Context, programme, construction and information all matter.

The practice builds on the same principles that have shaped Ricardo's architectural work to date: understand the context, test the programme, develop the architectural idea and carry it through with enough technical discipline to make it buildable.

BIM and digital delivery are used to strengthen that process — improving coordination, information quality and communication between designers, consultants, contractors and clients.

Experience

Design practice through to project delivery.

Experience includes architectural practice in Dublin and London, work across residential and technical design stages, and BIM / digital engineering roles in Australian construction and consultancy environments.

This combination allows STRINATI Design to support projects not only at concept and planning stage, but through detailed design, coordination, model review and digital delivery.
