Practice

Residential design and technical delivery with a clear, practical approach.

STRINATI Design is led by Ricardo Strinati and works with homeowners, small developers, builders and design teams, with a current residential focus in Sydney and experience developed across Ireland, the UK and Australia.

Approach

Understand what is possible before adding complexity.

The practice starts by understanding the site, brief, planning context and next decision. From there, design, documentation and coordination are developed to the level needed for the project to move forward with confidence.

This approach applies from site-potential reviews and planning work through to detailed documentation, Revit modelling and BIM coordination.

Experience

Architecture, construction and digital delivery.

Ricardo's experience spans architectural practice in Ireland and London, residential and technical design work, and BIM / digital-engineering roles in Australian construction and consultancy environments.

The result is a practice that understands both architectural intent and the information needed to coordinate and deliver a project effectively.
