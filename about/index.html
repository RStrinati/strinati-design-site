Meet Rico

One point of contact. A considered, practical approach.

STRINATI Design is led by Ricardo Strinati (Rico), an architectural designer based in Sydney’s Eastern Suburbs, with experience across Ireland, the UK and Australia.

Design thinking with technical depth

Rico’s background combines architectural practice in Ireland and London with digital engineering and delivery roles in Australian construction and consultancy. That experience informs the way he tests options, develops drawings and coordinates information.

Each commission is personally led by Rico. The work starts with the property, the brief and the next decision—not an assumed full-service appointment. The scope can grow in agreed stages where that is useful.

Education

  • BA (Hons) Architecture — University of Limerick.
  • Architectural Technology — Waterford Institute of Technology.
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Professional Practice — University College Dublin.

Experience and project credits

The selected UK and Irish projects show residential planning, concept development and technical coordination. Project pages identify location, status and contribution so the experience can be understood in context.

Where a project needs specialist planning, engineering, certification or other professional input, the required roles and appointments are identified as part of the agreed scope.
